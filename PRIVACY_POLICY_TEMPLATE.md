Receipt Archive helps users scan, organize, and export receipt and expense records. The current version processes receipt text on the device and stores receipt images and expense details in the app’s private local storage.

## Data handling

Receipt Archive does not transmit receipt images, extracted text, expense data, or identifiers to the developer. It does not include accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data-collection SDKs. Data leaves the app only when the user deliberately exports or shares a file using Apple’s system share interface; the privacy practices of the selected destination then apply.

## Camera and photos

Camera access is requested only when the user starts a receipt scan. Apple VisionKit is used to detect and crop the document. If image import is added or used, Apple’s system picker provides only the images selected by the user.

## Retention and deletion

Records remain on the user’s device until the user deletes them or removes the app. Deleting a matter removes the receipts and images associated with it. Device backups may be handled by the user’s Apple and device settings.
