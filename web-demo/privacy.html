Privacy policy

Effective 4 August 2026

ReceiptSure helps you scan, organize, and export receipt and expense records. Receipt text is processed on your device, and receipt images and expense details are stored in the app’s private local storage.

Data collection

ReceiptSure does not send receipt images, extracted text, expense data, or identifiers to the developer. This version has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data-collection SDKs.

Camera, photos, and files

Camera access is requested only when you start a receipt scan. Apple VisionKit detects and crops the document. Image and PDF imports use Apple’s system pickers, which provide only the items you select.

Security and backups

Optional app locking uses Apple’s device-owner authentication. Optional portable archives are encrypted locally with a password-derived key. ReceiptSure does not store or receive that password and cannot recover a forgotten one.

Exports and retention

Data leaves ReceiptSure only when you deliberately export, back up, or share a file using Apple’s system interfaces. The selected destination’s privacy practices then apply. In-app records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Exported files remain wherever you chose to save or share them.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated before introducing cloud sync, accounts, analytics, support uploads, or other developer-side processing. For questions, use the ReceiptSure support page.
