Help & support

ReceiptSure works privately on your iPhone by default. No ReceiptSure account is required.

Scanning

Place the receipt on a contrasting surface with even light. Hold the iPhone steady while the document frame detects the edges. Always compare the proposed merchant, date, currency, tax, and total with the original before saving.

Backups

Create a secure archive from Settings and save it somewhere you control. Keep its password separately: ReceiptSure cannot recover a forgotten archive password.

Private iCloud sync

In Settings, open Private iCloud sync and enable “Sync with private iCloud.” ReceiptSure will merge the newest receipt, matter, merchant-rule, and custom-category changes with devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Keep iCloud Drive and iCloud Keychain enabled, then tap “Sync now” after making important changes on another device.

To stop syncing, turn the option off. To remove ReceiptSure’s encrypted cloud snapshot while keeping all receipts on this iPhone, choose “Delete iCloud copy.” First disable sync on other devices if you do not want one of them to upload the library again.

Get assistance

Open a request in the project’s support tracker. Describe the device model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem.

Protect your privacy: do not attach real receipts, card details, addresses, tax identifiers, or archive passwords to a public support request.
